# Atelier — Stakeholders

Who the project serves — described by **role**, not individual names.

---

## Primary stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Who they are | What they need from Atelier |
|-------------|--------------|----------------------------|
| **Creators / studio operators** | Independent authors or small production studios running a **private** workspace (not an open multi-author marketplace) | AI-assisted drafting, revision, voice casting, publishing, and catalog management via `/studio` and `/library` |
| **Readers** | Paying customers on the **public catalog** | Discover serial fiction, preview free chapters, unlock paid installments, continue a series chapter by chapter |
| **Listeners** | Often the same people as readers | Read and listen in one flow — chapter text plus TTS audio in the player |

**Business model:** Creators publish **Pocket-FM-style serials**; readers/listeners pay per chapter (or preview free). Readers do **not** co-author inside the creator studio.

---

## Where & when

| Stakeholder | Primary surfaces |
|-------------|------------------|
| Creators / studio operators | `/studio`, `/library` (admin) |
| Readers & listeners | `/` (catalog), `/store` (unlocks), chapter player |

**When:** MVP stage — core creator and reader flows work end-to-end; production payments (Stripe) and native mobile are planned.
